Study tips

  • Try first, then ask. Attempt the problem for five minutes. You will learn more from the tutor’s steps.
  • Ask why. If a step is unclear, ask “why this step?” until it makes sense.
  • Answer the follow-up question. The tutor ends every solution with a mini-challenge. Solve it without help.
  • Send clear photos. Good light, no blur, the whole question in the frame.
  • Write essays yourself. The tutor gives an outline and an opening paragraph. You write the rest.
  • Review the next day. Redo yesterday’s problem from memory.
