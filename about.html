About

AI Tutor teaches in small steps: the idea first, then the working, then the answer, then a quick tip.

Good to know

  • The tutor’s answers come from Claude. Chat works only when the Chat page is opened inside Claude.
  • The other pages work anywhere, including when you open the files on your phone or computer.
  • The tutor can make mistakes. Check important answers with your teacher or textbook.
  • Chats are not saved. Refreshing the page clears them.
  • You can attach photos, PDFs with selectable text, and plain text files.
